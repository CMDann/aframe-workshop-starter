# Asset Attribution

**No assets are bundled yet.** The kit is populated in Phase 2 — see [README.md](README.md).

Every asset committed to `models/` or `skyboxes/` must have a row in the tables below before it is merged. This is not optional: an unattributed asset in a public repo is a legal problem waiting to surface, and participant scenes built from this kit may themselves be published publicly.

Each row needs the source URL, the exact license, and — for anything CC-BY — the required credit text captured verbatim rather than paraphrased.
