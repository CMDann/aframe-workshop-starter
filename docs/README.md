## Learning A-Frame

The [A-Frame guide](aframe/) goes well past what a 2-hour session can cover — for participants who want to keep building, and facilitators who want to answer questions rather than deflect them.

1. [Concepts](aframe/01-concepts.md) — scene graph, entities and components, why declarative
2. [Primitives](aframe/02-primitives.md) — the building blocks
3. [Transforms](aframe/03-transforms.md) — position, rotation, scale, and negative Z
4. [Lighting & materials](aframe/04-lighting-materials.md) — mood, and why your defaults vanished
5. [Models](aframe/05-models.md) — `.glb`, scaling, CORS
6. [Interactivity](aframe/06-interactivity.md) — cursor, events, animation, sound
7. [VR & devices](aframe/07-vr-and-devices.md) — headsets, comfort, performance
