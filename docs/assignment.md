# Assignment: Build a Basic WebXR Scene

## The task

Using A-Frame, build a basic 3D scene that reflects the mood or theme you picked in the Direction step. You can use assets from the supplied asset kit, find your own, or mix both.

## Requirements

Your finished scene needs:

- An environment: at least one sky or skybox (`a-sky`).
- At least one light (`a-light`), set deliberately — not just left at default if it works against your mood.
- At least 2-3 objects, placed with intention — position, scale, rotation, and color should all reflect a decision, not just "wherever it landed."
- The scene must load and enter VR mode from your shared link.

That's the floor, not the ceiling — if you finish early, see Stretch Goals below.

## Choosing your assets

**Option A — Use the supplied asset kit.** Fast, pre-vetted, already licensed and linked. Recommended if you're short on time or just want to focus on composition rather than asset-hunting.

**Option B — Find your own.** Good sources: Poly Pizza, Sketchfab (filter to downloadable models with a CC0 or CC-BY license), Poly Haven (for skybox/environment images). Rules if you go this route:

- The asset must be free-to-use — CC0 preferred, CC-BY acceptable if you credit the source.
- You must be able to download the actual file (`.glb`/`.gltf` for models) — a viewer-only page isn't usable.
- Note the source and license in a comment at the top of your scene file.
- **Give yourself 10 minutes, max.** If you haven't found something that works by then, fall back to the asset kit. The assignment is about scene design, not asset search.

Mixing both is fine — kit assets for some elements, your own for others.

## Deliverable

A live, shareable link (Glitch or equivalent) to your finished scene. It should load and let a viewer enter VR mode directly from that link — that's what "finished" means here.

## Stretch goals (optional)

If you finish with time to spare:

- Add interactivity — a click or gaze event that changes something in the scene.
- Layer in a second light for mood/contrast rather than flat, even lighting.
- Add ambient sound.

None of these are required. A complete, intentional basic scene beats an incomplete ambitious one.
